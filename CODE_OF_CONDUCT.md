# Code of Conduct

TrackPress follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive, and mindful that contributors have different levels of experience.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct issues privately through the repository's security contact. Maintainers may remove
content or participation that violates these expectations.
